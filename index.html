Generate a map of Substack subscribers

Where are your readers?

Use details from your Substack subscriber export to generate a shareable world map.

Click here to see the instructions for using this tool

Get country codes from Substack

  1. On your laptop or computer, log in to https://substack.com.
  2. On the left-hand panel, click Dashboard.
  3. On the left-hand panel, click Audience.
  4. Find the subscriber list in the table. Click the … at the top-right of the table.
  5. In the dropdown menu, click Export.
  6. In the popup, select Export all columns and click Export.
  7. After a few seconds, the button text will change to Download last export. Click it.
  8. Open the CSV in Excel and find the column titled Country (normally column M).

Generate the map

  1. Select and copy the values in the Country column from Excel (with or without the title), then paste them into the Subscriber country codes box below.
  2. Update Your home country and click Generate Map.

Search by country name or ISO country code.

Paste the cells from Excel’s Country column. A header row is fine; duplicates and unrecognized rows are skipped.

Your pasted data stays in this page and is not saved.

Your map is ready
